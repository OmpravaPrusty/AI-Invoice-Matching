Product Development Roadmap
AI Invoice Matching 

1. Purpose
This roadmap outlines the development phases, milestones, deliverables, and implementation strategy for Version 1 of the AI-Powered Purchase Order & Invoice Matching System.
The primary objective is to deliver a scalable Minimum Viable Product (MVP) capable of intelligently comparing Purchase Orders and Supplier Invoices using AI while maintaining a modular architecture for future expansion.

2. Development Strategy
The project follows an incremental development approach.
Each phase delivers a complete, testable feature before moving to the next phase. This minimizes development risk and allows continuous validation throughout the project lifecycle.

3. Success Criteria
Version 1 will be considered complete when the application can:
Authenticate users securely.
Upload Purchase Orders.
Upload Supplier Invoices.
Extract document information automatically.
Compare both documents intelligently.
Detect discrepancies accurately.
Generate AI-powered comparison summaries.
Store comparison history.
Generate downloadable reports.
