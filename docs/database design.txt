Database Design Document
AI Invoice Matching 

1. Overview
The database is designed to store users, vendors, uploaded Purchase Orders, uploaded Invoices, extracted document data, AI comparison results, and discrepancy reports.
The design follows normalization principles to avoid data duplication while remaining flexible for future enhancements such as Goods Receipt Notes (GRN), approval workflows, and ERP integration.

2. Database
Database: PostgreSQL (Supabase)
